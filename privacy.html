Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 15, 2026. This policy explains our steadfast commitment to privacy and data minimization.

Information We Collect

We practice extreme data minimization:

  • No Account Tracking: We do not require registration, passwords, phone numbers, or email addresses to chat.
  • Temporary Nicknames: Display names are chosen for the duration of the active chat session and are not stored in persistent profiles.
  • Push Subscription (Optional): If you choose to enable browser push notifications, anonymous push subscription tokens are used solely to alert you of incoming messages while the tab is inactive.

Use of Information

Any technical data processed during a session is used strictly to establish WebSocket connections, route messages to room members, and ensure platform health. We never sell, monetize, or track your personal data or conversation logs.

Security Practices

We implement robust transport security (HTTPS/WSS) and private password-protected rooms. Because rooms are peer-focused and lightweight, users should exercise sound discretion and avoid sending sensitive personal identifiers or financial information over public channels.
